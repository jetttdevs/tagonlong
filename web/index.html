Three steps, in order

No signup, no login, no wallet connection. Everything happens from your X account, and your fees wait at an address that is already yours.

01

Post the tag

Mention TagOnLong with a name in quotes and a ticker with a dollar sign. The parser is strict: one ticker, 2–10 characters, starting with a letter.

02

Get the reply

The bot deploys through Long.xyz and replies in your thread with the contract address and a link to the explorer. Usually inside a minute.

03

Claim your fees

Tag the bot with claim and the address you want paid. The post itself is the proof, because X tells the bot who sent it.

Your fees have an address before you do

Every account gets its own fee contract, derived from your X account id. It exists before you have done anything, which is why you can launch without connecting a wallet first. Your share goes straight there.

No private key controls that contract — not yours, and not ours. TagOnLong cannot withdraw from it, cannot redirect what is already in it, and cannot pay itself from it. To claim, you tag the bot with the address you want paid, then send one transaction from that wallet. Until then the fees sit there. Nothing expires.

If this site disappeared tomorrow, your token and your fees keep working. The contracts are permissionless, and nothing here can freeze, seize, or claw back a token that already exists.
